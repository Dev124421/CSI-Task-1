OVERVIEW

The One-Armed Wolf

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice is an action-adventure game set in the closing years of the Sengoku period. You play as a shinobi known as Wolf, bound to protect a young lord and reclaim what has been taken from you.

The game combines sword combat, stealth, vertical traversal and prosthetic tools into a focused single-player experience where timing and posture are central to every encounter.
